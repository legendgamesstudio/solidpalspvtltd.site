Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

SOLIDPALS PVT. LIMITED ("we", "us", "our") publishes mobile games on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used and shared when you use our applications ("Apps"), including:

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address or phone number. Our Apps may automatically collect limited, non-personal information through third-party services, including device model, operating system version, language, app usage statistics, crash logs and advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID).

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain and improve our Apps

3. Third-Party Services

Our Apps use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include:

  • Google Play Services

  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Crashlytics

Please review their privacy policies to understand how they handle your data.

4. Advertising

Our Apps are free and supported by ads. Advertising partners may use the advertising ID to show relevant ads and measure their performance. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Users in the EEA/UK will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

5. In-App Purchases

All payments are handled securely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data Security & Retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
